THE HIMALAYAN TRIAL OF INDRA

Welcome to the official tactical compendium for Swarajya 3D — an authentic 3D Real-Time Strategy game built on pure Three.js and deterministic lockstep simulation. Grounded in authentic Himalayan Siddhapedia lore and classic Warrior Kings: Battles tactical warfare, commanders clash across jagged snow ridges, cultivate barley fields, forge war chariots, and command mythical dragons.

🌾 1. Economy & Supply Lines

Swarajya operates on a physical, three-resource economy. Resources are not abstract numbers — they are harvested, held, and hauled across physical terrain.

🟡 Gold (Suvarna)

Mined by Praja (Peasants) from glowing mountain ore seams. Pays for all unit training, building construction, and weapon upgrades.

🌲 Timber (Daruka)

Felled by Praja from alpine cedar and pine forests. Required for raising building timber frames, watchtowers, and siege artillery.

🌾 Food (Dhanya)

Cultivated on Kshetra (Farms). Sustains your standing army. An army with zero food begins to suffer starvation morale loss.

📦 Supply Carts (Shakata)

Peasants drop loads at forward Kosha (Warehouses). Automated Carts must haul goods to your Asana (Manor) before they can be spent. Kill enemy carts to sever supply lines!

⌨️ 6. Controls & Hotkeys Quick Reference

Action Hotkey / Mouse Control Description
Camera Panning W A S D or Arrow Keys Smoothly glides the tactical camera across the battlefield.
Speed Acceleration Shift + WASD Accelerates camera panning speed by +50% for rapid navigation.
Center Camera Space Snaps camera directly over selected units, or returns to base if no selection.
Minimap Navigation Left-Click / Drag on Minimap Instantly jumps camera to that exact world coordinate.
Camera Orbit & Pitch Right-Click Drag or Middle-Click Drag Rotates panoramic yaw angle and tilts pitch from low vista to top-down.
Camera Zoom Mouse Scroll Wheel Smoothly zooms from close-up combat to high-altitude panoramic view.
Idle Peasant E or . Selects and focuses camera on the next unassigned idle peasant.
Tactical Chat Enter Opens scrollable match chat history. Press Tab to cycle [ALL] / [ALLIES] / [Whisper].
Diplomacy & Pacts P or F2 Toggles diplomacy modal to adjust Ally / Neutral / Enemy stances and send tributes.
Ctrl Multi-Build & Queue Hold Ctrl + Left/Right-Click Place multiple walls/farms in rapid succession without exiting build mode; queues movement and attack orders.
Control Groups Ctrl + 1–9 / 1–9 Ctrl+1..9 assigns selected squadron. Press 1..9 to recall. Double-tap to center camera on group.
Patrol & Guard Orders P (Patrol) / G (Guard) P + click sets continuous waypoint patrol route. G + click escorts and defends allied unit, hero, or structure.
Always-On Health Bars Alt or Tab Toggles continuous overhead health bars for all units and buildings across the battlefield.
Formations Cycle F Cycles selected squadron between Line (Pankti), Wedge (Garuda), Square (Vajra), and Scatter (Skirmish).
Combat Stances Z X C V Z Aggressive (pursue on sight) | X Defensive (guard post) | C Stand Ground (hold rank) | V Hold Fire (stealth).
Cancel / Close Menu Esc Clears current selection, cancels blueprint placement, or opens settings menu.

🌐 7. Serverless WebRTC Peer-to-Peer Multiplayer

Swarajya 3D features 100% serverless WebRTC multiplayer running directly in your browser:

  • Host a Room: Go to the 🌐 Online 1v1 tab and click Host Realm Room to receive a 5-letter code (e.g. KSET9).
  • Join a Room: Have your friend enter KSET9 and click Join Realm Room.
  • Global Direct P2P: Connects browsers directly over low-latency WebRTC DataChannels using Google STUN without requiring any backend servers!

📜 10. Single-Player Story Campaign (The Himalayan Chronicles)

Chapter I: The Mountain Pass of Trishul

Raiders descend from the high snowy crags to seize the ancestral pass. Command Senapati Indra, construct an Akhara (Barracks), survive 3 raiding assaults, and vanquish the Raider Chieftain in the northern heights.

Objectives: [1] Build an Akhara • [2] Survive 3 Raiding Waves • [3] Defeat Raider Chieftain

Chapter II: Trial of the 8-Headed Naga

Rogue cultists have desecrated the sacred mountain terrace. Guide Kaula Acharya, train 4 Yoginis at your Akhara, harness the celestial Vajra Storm, and cleanse the sacred Naga Shrine.

Objectives: [1] Train 4 Yoginis • [2] Cast Vajra Storm 2 Times • [3] Cleanse Sacred Shrine

Chapter III: The Siege of Kailash Sanctum

The mountain citadel of the usurper sits heavily fortified behind stone walls and watchtowers. Assemble a siege train of Catapults and Rams to conquer the mountain palace.

Objectives: [1] Upgrade Asana to Keep • [2] Erect 2 Catapults or Rams • [3] Destroy Mountain Citadel

Chapter IV: Night Ambush in the Pine Passes

Night falls across the snow ridges. Mountaineer raiders use the fog for cover. Mount archers upon stone battlements (+50% Range), take the Surya Tirtha to illuminate the ridge, and defeat the Night Warlord.

Objectives: [1] Mount 4 Archers on Stone Walls • [2] Capture Surya Tirtha • [3] Vanquish Night Warlord

Chapter V: Defense of the Five River Bridges

The glacial river divides the realms. Hostile war chariots and battering rams attempt to force the river crossings. Construct fortified gatehouses (Dwara) to bar passage, deploy War Chariots (Ratha), and repel 4 massive crossing assaults.

Objectives: [1] Construct 2 Gatehouses • [2] Train 2 War Chariots (Ratha) • [3] Repel 4 River Crossing Waves

Chapter VI: Grand Finale — Siege of the Asura Citadel

The ultimate showdown against the Asura tyrant nestled in the high caldera. Consecrate all 4 Sacred Himalayan Tirthas to shatter the tyrant's mystical wards, erect 3 Heavy Catapults, and raze the Mountain Palace to claim eternal Swarajya.

Objectives: [1] Consecrate All 4 Sacred Tirthas • [2] Erect 3 Heavy Catapults • [3] Raze Asura Mountain Palace
