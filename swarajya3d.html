🏰 Economy & Resources

🤝 Diplomacy & In-Game Chat
  • Diplomacy (P): Press P or F2 or click header button to set Ally 🤝, Neutral ⚖️, or Enemy ⚔️ stances with each realm.

🎮 Controls & Navigation
  • Selection: Left-Click or Drag a box around units. Click Manor to train peasants.
  • Orders: Right-Click ground to move, right-click enemy to attack, right-click gold/wood to assign.
  • Camera Pan: Use WASD or Arrow Keys. Hold Shift for +50% Speed Boost.
  • Panoramic Zoom: Scroll mouse wheel to zoom from ground view to high mountain vista.
  • Orbit Camera: Hold Right/Middle mouse button and drag to rotate angles.
  • Quick Idle Worker: Press E or . to focus next idle peasant.
